Getting started

Three steps.
That's all.

STEP 01

Log in with Realm

Click "Get Scripts" and you'll be sent to sign in with your school Realm account. This verifies you're a student.

STEP 02

Copy the loader

After signing in, you'll get a short piece of code. Copy it — it's the only thing you need to run any script.

STEP 03

Paste into the console

On Realm or EP, press Ctrl + Shift + J to open the console, paste the code, and hit Enter. Done.

  1. Pressing Continue will redirect you to log in with Realm.
